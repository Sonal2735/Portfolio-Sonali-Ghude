An IoT food-freshness monitoring prototype, built by a team I led.

Status: prototype. My role: team lead.

What I did

Robotics · soft robotics · sustainable design

Robospire

A bio-inspired, modular soft spiral robotic arm, modelled on an elephant’s trunk and an octopus tentacle.

Conventional industrial arms struggle with objects of different sizes and shapes, tangled wiring, installation effort and power failures. Robospire takes a softer approach: the arm wraps around an object and holds it gently, using a simple tendon-based mechanism with very few sensors.

  • A small backup module releases the object if power fails, to avoid damage or accidents.
  • Automatic homing returns the arm to its starting position for a quick restart.
  • 3D-printed from recyclable TPU and resin. The modular design lets parts be replaced or extended without removing the whole arm.
  • Target settings: manufacturing, warehouses, healthcare and drone-based operations.

My role: team lead.
